On pause

Steady is taking a break.

Steady was an AI cycling coach. Most training apps build your plan around FTP, a single number under load. We used LT1, your aerobic threshold, because it's what actually determines how you hold up over hours in the saddle. Each plan was built around your goal's specific demands, not a generic template.

We're pausing it. Getting it right takes more time than we can give it right now, so we'd rather stop and come back when we can do it properly.

If that day comes, you will be the first to know.
